Backup · Questions

Questions you may have

What leaves the machine today?

Our filtered messages, plus what the AI reads inside the folders we mount for it, sent over an encrypted connection to the model provider. So only approved material goes into those folders.

What if the AI is wrong?

The same as any draft: it is checked, simulated, tested and signed off by a qualified engineer. Nothing goes live on its word.

Can it act on its own?

Only inside its container, only on folders we mount, and only past automatic gates that block risky actions before they run.

Who uses it?

The study team, on non-confidential work, while we build the evidence.

Is our data used for training?

No. We are on a business (Team) plan. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, inputs and outputs are not used for training by default, and Anthropic acts as our data processor.

How do you know the controls work?

We test them. The harness is audited by a separate read-only process, and every finding comes with a repeatable proof.
